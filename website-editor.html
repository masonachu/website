Website editor

Edits the parts of the site that change most often, and writes them straight back into the page files. Everything else in each file is left exactly as it is.

  • Reels — the Vimeo videos at the top of the home page and each portfolio page.
  • Bio — the “hello!” text and the photo behind it on the home page.
  • Highlighted projects — the tiles on the home page: photo, name, role, link, order, big/small.
  • Quotes & photo — the quotes band on each portfolio page and its background photo.
  • Players — the SoundCloud / YouTube / Bandcamp cards on the composition pages.
  • Videos — the sound design clips: title, poster, streaming link and local MP4.
  • Projects — the project cards on the programming page: write-up, role, tools, video or image, button.
  • Write-up — long-form project pages like Wartorn: intro, then chapters of paragraphs, image rows and videos.
  • Contact — the email card on the contact page: note, address and social links.

Open website folder is the easy route (Chrome or Edge): pick your website folder once and the tool loads every page, saves them back and copies new photos into images/ for you.

Load pages works in any browser, including Safari: pick one or more of the .html files. Saving then downloads new copies for you to drop into the folder, and you copy any new photos into images/ yourself using the filenames shown.

New page (bottom of the left column) builds a page from the ones already loaded: pick its sections in order and it gets the same header, footer and styling, plus a link in the menu on every page. + Add section under any page does the same for one section.

After saving, commit and push as usual.
